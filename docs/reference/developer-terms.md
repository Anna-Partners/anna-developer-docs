3. does not attempt to bypass Platform security controls, sandbox restrictions, review processes, rate limits, or billing;
4. complies with applicable laws and does not contain illegal, hateful, or deceptive content;
5. accurately describes what it does — listings, manifests, and requested permissions must match the actual behavior of the app.

2. Published versions may additionally be subject to release review before going live.
3. The Platform may remove, archive, or block any app or version that violates these terms or poses a risk to users, without prior notice.
